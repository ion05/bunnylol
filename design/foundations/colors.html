Every token in tokens.css, with the contrast ratio it was chosen for. The accent exists as two tokens because #e1ab76 is 2.04:1 on white and cannot legally be text, a focus ring, or a state indicator in light mode.

Surfaces

--bg-raised #fafaf9 Hover fill.

--border #e5e5e2 1.26:1 — a hairline separator, never the edge of a control.
--border-strong #d3d3ce 1.5:1 — heavier separator, disabled button edge.
--control-border #8a8a82 Control edges. 3.10:1 on --bg-sunken light, 4.07:1 dark — clears WCAG 1.4.11.

--text-decor #a3a39c Decorative marks only — the status dot, the preview arrow. Exempt from text contrast.
Accent
--accent #e1ab76 Fill only. 2.04:1 on white — never text, never a ring, never a state indicator.
--accent-hover #d69b5d Hover fill for the primary button.
--accent-text #895420 The readable half. 6.26:1 light, 9.11:1 dark on --bg. Same hue and saturation at half lightness.
--accent-fg #1a1a1a Text on the sand fill: 8.53:1. White would be 2.04:1.
