Accent — one colour, two tokens

#e1ab76 is 2.04:1 on white, so it cannot legally be text, a focus ring or a state indicator in light mode. --accent-text is the same hue and saturation at half lightness (6.26:1); in dark the raw sand is already 9.11:1, so dark needs no separate value.

--accent Fill only. Primary button, switch track, icon.
--accent-hover Primary button hover.
--accent-text Links, the keyword mark, the nav underline, the focus ring.
--accent-fg Text on the sand fill: 8.53:1. White would be 2.04:1.
--ok 5.45 light, 8.39 dark.
--warn 5.75 light, 8.27 dark.
--danger 5.96 light, 6.77 dark.
--danger-fg Armed confirm button only.

Buttons

Four families, one filled. Disabled dims by colour, not opacity — opacity: 0.5 also fades the border below 3:1 and cannot be measured. The focus ring is shown forced on.

Inputs and controls

A switch means “this thing is on or off” (a shortcut). A checkbox means “include this in a set” (which engines to intercept, which packs to enable). They are not interchangeable.

A keyword cannot contain a space. Separate keywords with a comma.

Status and messages

Text plus a 6px dot — no capsule, no background, no radius. Messages carry no coloured bar and no icon: colour plus weight is the tone, the wording is the meaning.

Chips and badges

Keyword chips are outlined mono. Badges are lowercase and untinted; “off” and “omnibox only” keep an outline so they stay a visible non-colour marker.
