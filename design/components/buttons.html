Buttons

Four families, one filled. Sizes are set by --btn-h / --btn-h-sm, never by padding alone. The focus ring is shown forced on; in the product it appears on :focus-visible only.

Light
Families
Primary is the only filled button on a page. Sand fill with #1a1a1a text is 8.53:1; white on sand would be 2.04:1.
Armed — the second press of a destructive action
→
confirmButton arms on the first click and commits on the second. The red fill is the confirmation state, not decoration — nothing else on the page is filled red.
Small — row actions only
Disabled — dimmed by colour, not opacity
opacity: 0.5 also fades the border below 3:1 and cannot be measured. The disabled state sets colour and border-color instead.
Focus — one global ring, --accent-text
6.26:1 in light, 9.11:1 in dark. The raw sand would have been 2.04:1 and illegal as a focus indicator.
